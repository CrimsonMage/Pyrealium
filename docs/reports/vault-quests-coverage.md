## Method and limits

- Steps come from regular expressions over the walkthrough prose (talk, kill N X, loot, give, use, travel); quoted dialogue is excluded from step parsing. A prose shape the patterns miss leaves the quest partial or under-gapped, never shipped. `data complete` means every field the parser found resolved; it is not a review of the walkthrough.
- Names resolve through `content/definitions.tsv` and the DAT's own name strings (key 0xa8607cec of the entity's `.stt` table). Kill targets prefer the DAT's death effects that name the quest's journal id (`native-quests.json` entity bindings, confidence high); otherwise same-named entities placed in Pyrealium's world (medium) or any same-named entity (low).
- Journal ids: the DAT quest list by name; 7230 keeps most quest names in private tables, so the quest package (`QuestDarkstone`) is matched too, exactly or by its words (medium).
- NPC placements: Pyrealium's world placements of a same-named entity within 1.0 map units (about 200 m) of the Vault's start coordinates; interior cells use their landblock's centre and 1.8 units.
- Quotes: 7230 string-table text by FTS phrase, then similarity >= 0.75 (high >= 0.9, or the Vault quoting part of the line); localisation copies (`_english.stt_ansi` and others) are skipped.
- Effects: names of two words or more of `eff_*.stt` tables (key 0xa8607cec) found in the prose with their capitals, with their `eff_*.wc` WSTATE. A quest that mentions a curse/buff/effect without a name match still counts under ApplyEffect.
- Gaps come from the Vault's fields (level, time limit, reset timer, reward) and prose keywords; the keyword gaps are conservative (a mention of a fellowship or a chest counts).
